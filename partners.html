05 / A few answers

Clarity,
before you begin.

Understand the partner journey, who can join and what patients will see.

How does CuraX help my practice?

CuraX is being built to connect discovery with the workflows that follow: enquiries, bookings, visit preparation, payments, records and follow-up. Availability will depend on your facility and the enabled product scope.

How long does onboarding take?

The timeline depends on your category, profile completeness, required documents and verification. The team will confirm the next steps and expected timeline after reviewing your request.

Can an independent doctor join?

Independent doctors can express interest through their clinic or practice. The facility and practitioner information intended for patients must meet the applicable verification requirements.

What do patients see in the User App?

Patients see the approved projection of your facility, doctor and service information. Entering or changing details in Partner does not automatically publish them to the User App.

Are all the listed features available now?

No. The workflow areas shown here describe the planned provider experience. Current availability, activation and applicable commercial terms will be confirmed during onboarding.

How do I become a CuraX Partner?

Request an invite with your facility and contact details. The current form prepares an email for you to review and send to the CuraX team. It does not create a provider account.
